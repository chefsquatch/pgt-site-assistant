# How to Work With PGT — and How to Reach Les

## How engagements start
The way to engage PGT is by email. The founder, Les Fleming, reads the problem
and takes it from there. A good first message brings the actual problem — the
drift you're seeing, a build idea, or a stalled prototype — rather than a vague
"do you do X." The more concrete the problem, the faster Les can tell you whether
and how PGT can help.

## How Les works
- **Async and text-first.** PGT is built around messages and results, not
  meetings. Les does not do phone calls or sales calls as the default; the work
  and the communication happen in writing.
- **Small studio, hands-on.** You are working with the person doing the build,
  not an account manager routing you to a team.
- **Response time:** PGT states it gets back within a business day.

## The single contact channel
Email: **lesfleming@precisionguessworktech.com**

There is no booking/scheduling system, no phone line, and no live-chat-to-human
on the site. This assistant is the front door; email is how you actually reach
Les. If you're a fit and ready, the best thing to do is send him a message with
your problem — this assistant can help you summarize it first so he engages
already knowing your situation.

## What makes a good fit
PGT is a fit when the problem is in its wheelhouse: AI reliability (drift /
hallucination in an AI coding workflow), AI integration (Claude API, RAG, tool
use), a custom app or MVP build, or local-first / privacy-focused software. If a
problem is clearly outside that — unrelated to software, or a domain PGT doesn't
work in — the honest thing is to say so rather than pretend it's a fit.
