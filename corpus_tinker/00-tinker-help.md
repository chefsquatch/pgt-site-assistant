# Tinker help: how Tinker works

Tinker is staff time tracking for small businesses, made by Precision Guesswork Technologies (PGT). It tracks HOURS, not pay. Tinker never handles wages, pay rates, taxes, checks, or bank details. Payroll stays in the business's payroll tool; Tinker gives them accurate hours to put into it.

Each business has its own private workspace at tinker.precisionguessworktech.com/their-workspace-name. One business can never see another business's data.

There are two kinds of people in Tinker:
- Admins (owners and managers). They sign in with an email and password and manage everything below.
- Employees. They do not have accounts or passwords. They clock in and out with a personal passkey.

## Signing in (admins)

- Go to tinker.precisionguessworktech.com and click "Admin sign in", then enter your email and password. You land in your workspace.
- Forgot your password: on the sign-in page click "Forgot password?", enter your email, and click "Email me a reset link". Open the email "Reset your Tinker password" and choose a new password. The link works once and expires after 1 hour. Check spam if it does not arrive.
- To sign out, click "Sign out" at the bottom of the menu.

## The admin menu

Main pages: Roster, Schedule, Hours report, Correct a punch.
Settings pages: Share clock-in, Roles, Locations, Devices, PGT support.

## Roster (who is working)

- The Roster shows everyone, who is on the clock right now, hours today, and hours this pay period.
- Add an employee: click "+ Add employee", enter their name and email, then "Generate join link". Copy the link and send it to the new hire. They open it, confirm their name and email, and choose their own passkey (typed twice). The link works once and expires.
- Manage an employee: click "Manage" on their row. From there you can assign roles or remove them.
- Remove an employee: in Manage, click "Remove". Removed employees can no longer clock in, but their past hours stay on reports. They appear under "Former employees".
- Bring someone back: under Former employees, click "Restore". They can clock in again with their same passkey.
- Admins never set or see an employee's passkey. If an employee forgets theirs, remove and restore does not reset it; send them a new join link from "+ Add employee" so they can choose a new passkey, or contact PGT support.

## How employees clock in and out

- Employees go to the business's clock page (tinker.precisionguessworktech.com/workspace-name), type their passkey, and tap "Clock in" or "Clock out".
- If they don't know the workspace address, they can go to tinker.precisionguessworktech.com, tap "Clock in", enter their work email, and Tinker takes them to their business's clock.
- Tip for employees: add the clock page to the phone's home screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen) so next time it is one tap.
- If the business uses roles and the employee has more than one, they pick which role they are working when they clock in.
- A shared tablet works as a time clock: leave the clock page open on it, and each person types their own passkey.

## Share clock-in

- The Share clock-in page has your clock link and a QR code. Print the QR or show it to your team. They scan it once, save Tinker to their home screen, and clock in from then on.
- Each person still needs their own passkey (from their join link).

## Locations (where people clocked in)

- On Locations, click "Add location", give it a name, and either "Use my current location" while standing there or type the address. Each location has a radius.
- When an employee allows location, each clock-in and clock-out is tagged with the matching location, or "off-site" if it is outside every location. For off-site punches Tinker shows a nearby street address.
- Location is optional for employees. The first time, the clock page explains what is recorded and asks; they can tap "Allow location" or "Not now". If they decline, their clock still works.
- Location tags are visible to the business's admins on the Correct a punch page. Tinker never sells location data.
- If an employee tapped allow but nothing happens, their browser has blocked location for the site. They can turn it on in the site permissions (the lock icon in the address bar) and reload.

## Devices (named tablets)

- On Devices, add a device name (for example "Food truck iPad"), then "Copy setup link" and open that link once on the tablet. After that, every punch made on that tablet is tagged with its name.
- "Deactivate" retires a device without losing its history; "Reactivate" brings it back.

## Roles

- On Roles, create roles your team works (for example Lead, Catering Help, Prep). Rename or "Deactivate" them any time; history is kept.
- Assign roles to each person from the Roster ("Manage"). When clocking in, employees pick the role they are working, and the role shows on punches and schedules.

## Schedule

- On Schedule, pick the week, then "Add shift" for each person: day, start and end time, and optionally a role, a location (where the shift is), and a note. "Save schedule" when done.
- Import and export: "Export" downloads the week as a CSV you can open in Excel or Google Sheets. "Import CSV" lets you paste or load a CSV with these columns: Employee, Day, Start, End, Role, Location, Note. Rows that cannot be matched are skipped and listed.
- You can also upload a schedule file you made elsewhere.
- "Send to roster" emails the current schedule to your active employees.

## Hours report and pay periods

- On Hours report, first set your pay period: Weekly, Biweekly, or Semimonthly. Weekly and biweekly also need the first day of a period. The report then shows hours per person for the period, with previous and next period buttons.
- Hours always come from the actual clock-in and clock-out times; they are never typed in.
- "Email report" sends the current period's report to you.
- Check "Auto-email this report to admins when each period closes" to get it automatically after every period.
- Corrected punches are flagged in the report.

## Correct a punch

- On Correct a punch, find the punch and edit its clock-in or clock-out time (for example when someone forgot to clock out), then "Save correction".
- Tinker records which admin made the correction and when. Corrections cannot be made by PGT support; they must come from the business's own admin.

## PGT support access

- If you want PGT to look into a problem inside your workspace, go to Settings, then "PGT support", and click "Allow for 24 hours" or "Allow for 7 days". Click "Turn off now" any time.
- PGT can never open your workspace unless this is on. Every visit is listed under Visit history on that page.

## Signing up a new business

- A new business creates its own workspace at tinker.precisionguessworktech.com/signup by choosing a workspace name and creating the first admin account.

## Privacy

- Tinker's privacy policy is at tinker.precisionguessworktech.com/privacy. Location is optional, visible only to the business's admins, and never sold.

## Not covered here

- Pricing, plans, billing, and contracts are handled by PGT directly. The assistant does not quote prices.
- Bugs, errors, data that looks wrong, account problems it cannot solve with the steps above, or anything not described here go to PGT support.
