The assistant that won't make things up.

This is PGT's site assistant. It answers only from what PGT actually offers, and it tells you plainly when something's outside that — then points you to the founder. It demonstrates the reliability PGT sells by being it.

Try to catch it fabricating

  1. Ask something real: What does PGT do about AI drift?
  2. Force it: Exactly how much is a custom app? — it won't invent a price.
  3. Off-topic: What's the capital of Australia? — it declines and steers back.
  4. Describe a real problem and let it summarize and hand you off to email.
